# 1.2.0 close-up workshop — implementation report

This development report records what was built for `Immersive_Brewing_Cinematic_Hands_On_Overhaul_Handoff.md` (the
close-up camera, direct setup, continuous transfers and material feedback on top of the 1.2.0 hands-on workshop), how
it was checked and what remains open. It is not a release announcement. Results are stated only for checks that were
run; everything that was not run is listed under [Pending gates](#pending-gates).

## Feature map

What the overhaul kept, replaced, removed or left optional (handoff 16, item 6).

| Area | Status | What it is now | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Working view, part volumes, intents, leases, single tick owner, attended batches, sealing, recovery (ADR-5) | Retained | Unchanged authority model; every action is still validated from the player's eyes | The handoff replaces presentation, not the workshop |
| Camera framing ("focus" turned the player's view) | Replaced | `WorkshopCamera` moves the render camera to an authored, fitted close-up and back (`CameraMixin`), with inspect, obstruction fallback and three styles | The old framing could not show into the vessel (handoff 5) |
| `cameraMode` option | Retained as legacy | Read only while `cameraStyle` is `AUTO`; `DIRECT` resolves to `EYE` | A player who chose not to have a moving camera keeps that choice (handoff 5.5) |

| Station menus (Kettle, Mortar, Alembic, Rack) | Retained as optional maintenance access | Opened with the drawer key (`I`) from the working view; not needed in any journey; the client now builds their slot layout before the block entity arrives | Optional access must still be stable (handoff 12.4) |

| Click-to-pour / click-to-dose | Replaced | A carry: lift, travel over the mouth, align, tip, flow, return, settle; ownership moves once when the server accepts the tip | Continuous transfers (handoff 7.3) |
| Batch actions (pour, fill, take, place all) | Retained, re-paced | One server-paced transfer at a time with a lead time, so each visible lift ends as it commits | The batch shortcut must not look like teleporting stacks (handoff 7.3) |
| Hotbar, health and food bars hidden while working | Replaced | Only the crosshair, experience bar and jump bar are hidden; the hotbar is the supply strip, health and food stay visible, and the camera fit keeps a band free for them | Players must not be unaware of danger (handoff 10) |
| Words on screen (title bar, cue panel, key legend) | Replaced | Guidance levels: the hovered part's short label always; the next useful action and first-use demonstrations when guided; numbers and the full control help (`H`) when detailed; refusals and warnings always | The default interface budget (handoff 10) |

| `presentation` field of workshop profiles | Replaced (was reserved, no effect) | Names a client presentation profile (resource packs) | Presentation profiles (handoff 12.1) |
| Kettle, Mortar and Alembic models | Replaced | Recomposed for the close-up (Kettle: wider opening, rail in front, tray and book left, paddle right; Alembic: boiler, vapour path, condenser, diverter, receiver, separate waste crock); block shapes, render bounds and part volumes changed with them | Recompose before polishing effects (handoff 4) |
| Alembic reclamation, started from the station menu's button | Added at the apparatus; the menu button retained | The idle fitting (`A_MODE`) chooses purification or reclamation and shows the other's outcome before anything is used; committed only when charged | Choosing reclamation through physical controls (handoff 9.2, 15.3 item 5) |
| Player arm | Added | Drawn in the player's skin at the grip of the held tool, bottle or cup (`hands = ARMS`), or the object alone (`TOOLS_ONLY`) | Player hands (handoff 7.4) |
| Neighbouring stations | Added | `N` moves the view to a station within three blocks through that station's own use checks; held tools and carries are released first | Workshop continuity (handoff 9.3) |
| Diegesis | Not adopted; camera hook adapted | ADR-6 | A second scene and authority engine and a beta hard dependency for one camera hook |
| Removed | Nothing player-facing was removed | | |

## Diegesis (handoff 11.1)

Diegesis now publishes 1.0.0-beta.8 for Minecraft 1.20.1 on Forge, so the artifact objection recorded for the first
1.2.0 overhaul no longer holds. It was still not adopted: it is a complete scene, input and station-authority engine,
and Brewing's server-owned parts, intents, leases and processes are what every station, test and saved job depends
on; running both would give one click two authorities, and it would be a beta, hard client-and-server dependency for a
single camera hook. Its camera mechanism (placing the camera at the end of `Camera.setup`, after vanilla and camera
mods) was adapted for `CameraMixin`. Diegesis is MIT-licensed (Copyright (c) 2026 Pocky); the notice ships in the jar
as `META-INF/licenses/Diegesis-MIT.txt`. See ADR-6 in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Known limitations

- The fuel hatch is a small target from the close-up camera: about 7 % of its projected box is pickable (the bottles in
  front cover the rest). Every journey placed the fuel there, but it is the smallest target the journeys point at.
- Sound was not redesigned in this pass. The existing 1.2.0 sounds stay; the stirring loop already follows the
  circulation the paddle produces and loops are capped at six voices. Material-specific placement, pour and glass
  sounds were not added.
- Rack: there is no carrier packing animation; the hovered bottle's label turn was added late and was not captured or
  reviewed in a screenshot. Take-one, take-matching, pack and unpack were already distinct actions with their
  quantities.
- The observer view's second brewer is a server-side fake player. It is not a listed player, so no body or arm is
  drawn for it; observers see the carried objects, paddle, liquid and filled bottles it moves. Two real clients remain
  a pending gate.
- The camera's obstruction alternatives and eye fallback are covered by the fit's unit tests and by code review; walls,
  ceilings, adjacent stations, third-person entry and exit, altered player field of view and ultrawide windows were not
  driven in the client.
- The journeys' player is a script. It decides from the synced snapshot what to do next, like the reference tenders,
  and acts only through the window's input. It proves the path works through real input, not that a person finds it
  pleasant or learnable.
- Hands-on batches set out before this change keep process version 1 rules (no posture).

## Pending gates

Not run, and not passes (handoff 15.3 items 9-10, 15.4, 16 item 8):

- **Human playtests:** a first-time player completing the guided first batch without coaching; an experienced
  player's repeated batches (fatigue, dead time, whether the Mortar's folding feels frequent); whether players can say
  why they slowed the paddle or changed the heat from what the apparatus showed. No person has played this build.
- **Two real clients on a dedicated server:** shared tools, separate controls, a late observer, a helper
